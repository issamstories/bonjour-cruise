Legal

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026.

1. Who we are

Bonjour Cruise ("we", "us") arranges shared yacht charters in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. Charters are operated by our licensed marine partner. This policy explains how we handle personal data collected through bonjourcruise.com, your member account, WhatsApp and our booking process, in line with UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data.

If you contact us or book from the European Union or the European Economic Area, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies to your data as well. Section 8 sets out what that means for you.

2. Data we collect

  • Inquiry data: name, WhatsApp number, email, preferred date, experience type, group size, occasion and special requests you submit through our forms or WhatsApp.
  • Member account data: if you create an account, we store your name, nickname, email, password (encrypted, never visible to us), WhatsApp number, city, nationality, date of birth, languages you speak, your cruise registrations and your sharing preferences. Date of birth is used only to group shared cruises by age band and, with your consent, to show an age to co-passengers.
  • Booking data: charter details, payment confirmations (processed by our payment provider, we do not store card numbers), and guest count for safety manifests.
  • Feedback data: the rating and comments you choose to send us after a cruise, linked to your account so we can improve.
  • Technical data: we use only the essential cookies needed to keep the site working. We do not run analytics, advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

2b. What other guests can see (shared cruises)

On a shared "by the seat" cruise, you can choose to let the other guests booked on that same departure see your first name, your age and the languages you speak. These options are off by default, nothing is shared unless you switch them on, and you can change them at any time. Other guests never see your full name, email, phone number, exact date of birth or city, and your details are only ever visible to guests booked on the same cruise as you.

3. How we use it

  • To prepare quotes, confirm bookings and operate your charter safely.
  • To meet maritime safety obligations (passenger manifests required by DMCA regulations).
  • To send you information you have asked for. We do not sell data and we do not send marketing without consent.

4. Photography and your image

Consistent with our Privacy Promise: photos taken by our optional on-board photographer are delivered to you privately and deleted from our systems after delivery. We never use guest images in marketing.

5. Sharing and processors

Booking details are shared only with the crew operating your charter, our halal-certified caterer (dietary needs only), and authorities where maritime law requires it. We use trusted processors to run the service: Netlify (website hosting and inquiry forms), Supabase (secure storage of member accounts and cruise data), Brevo (transactional and notification emails) and Stripe (payment processing; your card details are handled directly by Stripe and never reach our servers). These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and data-protection commitments. We never sell your data or share it publicly.

6. Retention

Inquiry data is kept for 12 months, and booking and safety records for 5 years as required by UAE regulations. Member account data is kept for as long as your account is active; you can delete your account, and all the personal data attached to it, at any time by writing to us.

7. Your rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of your personal data at any time by writing to info@bonjourcruise.com. We respond within 30 days.

8. If you are in the European Union or EEA (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Union or the European Economic Area when you contact us or book a cruise, the EU General Data Protection Regulation applies to your personal data in addition to UAE law. Bonjour Cruise is the data controller for everything described in this policy.

Legal bases we rely on

  • Performance of a contract, Article 6(1)(b): handling your booking, taking payment, preparing the passenger manifest and running your cruise.
  • Legal obligation, Article 6(1)(c): the maritime safety manifests and the booking records UAE regulations require us to keep.
  • Consent, Article 6(1)(a): showing your first name, age or spoken languages to the other guests on your cruise, and any marketing email. These are off by default, and you can withdraw consent at any time without affecting what was done before.
  • Legitimate interests, Article 6(1)(f): replying to your inquiry, keeping the site secure and preventing fraud. We weigh these against your rights, and you can object at any time.

Your rights under Articles 15 to 22

  • Access, Article 15: a copy of the personal data we hold about you and an explanation of what we do with it.
  • Rectification, Article 16: correction of anything inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Erasure, Article 17: deletion of your data, apart from the records maritime safety and accounting law oblige us to keep.
  • Restriction, Article 18: a pause on processing while a dispute about accuracy or lawfulness is resolved.
  • Data portability, Article 20: the data you gave us, in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format.
  • Objection, Article 21: an objection to any processing we base on our legitimate interests.
  • Automated decisions, Article 22: we take no automated decision producing legal or similarly significant effects about you, and we do not profile you.

To exercise any of these rights, write to the contact point in section 9. We answer within one month, as Article 12(3) requires, and exercising your rights costs you nothing.

Transfers outside the European Union

We operate from Dubai, United Arab Emirates, a country covered by no European Commission adequacy decision. Your data therefore leaves the EEA when you book with us. We rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses for those transfers, and the processors named in section 5 commit to the same clauses or to equivalent safeguards in their own data-processing terms. Ask us and we will send you a copy of the safeguards that apply to your data.

Complaints

If you think we have mishandled your data, tell us first so we can put it right quickly. You also have the right, under Article 77, to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority of the EU or EEA country where you live, where you work, or where you believe the problem occurred. Doing so takes nothing away from any other remedy open to you.

Why you see no cookie banner

We show no cookie banner because there is nothing to ask your consent for. This site runs no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking cookies. The only browser storage we use is strictly necessary to deliver what you asked for: your signed-in session and your language choice. Under Article 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive, strictly necessary storage needs no consent. If we ever add analytics or advertising, a consent banner will appear before any such script is allowed to run.

9. Contact

Bonjour Cruise, Dubai Marina, Dubai, United Arab Emirates, info@bonjourcruise.com.

For anything about your personal data, including the GDPR rights above, write to that same address with "Data request" in the subject line. It reaches the person responsible for data protection here.
