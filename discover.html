Why we exist

The sea belongs to everyone

Why should sailing solo mean sitting it out? Why should a good day on the water depend on already knowing enough people to fill a boat? Bonjour Cruise exists so that anyone, on their own, with family or with their closest friends, can swim, laugh and simply exhale, with a warm crew and a well-vetted crowd, nothing to watch for. The sea was never meant to leave anyone on the shore. Come as one, leave with friends, that is the whole idea.

Questions

Everything you want to know

Who makes up the crew?

Captain, hosts, photographer, spa therapists, every professional on board is trained, vetted and held to the same warm standard. It is the founding rule of Bonjour Cruise and we have never made an exception.

How private is a shared cruise?

Very. That is exactly what our privacy protocol is for. Once we are underway with curtained mooring in place, the deck is a relaxed, easy space, swim, dance, let go of the day.

Is the food halal, and is there alcohol?

You choose. Every cruise comes in a halal version (certified catering, an alcohol-free table and a prayer space) or a classic version. We never serve alcohol ourselves; on a classic or private cruise you are welcome to bring your own.

Is Bonjour Cruise only for Muslim guests?

Not at all. Bonjour Cruise is for everyone. The constant is a warm, vetted crew and full privacy; halal catering, a prayer space and an alcohol-free table are options you choose, never requirements. Muslim or not, solo, with friends or with your family, you are welcome.

Can I bring my partner or male friends, and can we sail as a family?

Yes. Bonjour Cruise is a mixed, social brand, everyone aged 18 and over is welcome. Our private charters welcome whoever you choose, including mixed family groups with children, and the crew keeps the same warm standard so everyone on board is completely at ease.

Can I book just for myself, or come alone?

Yes, that is exactly what our by-the-seat option is for. Reserve a single seat on a scheduled cruise and pay only for yourself instead of the whole yacht, from AED 380 per seat (launch offer). Come solo or bring a friend; you will sail with a handful of other guests, and most guests leave with new ones. It is still a warm, respectful atmosphere with the same crew, halal or classic, and the same privacy protocol. Shared departures sail once a small minimum of guests is reached, if a date does not fill, we move you to the next one or refund you in full.

How does pricing work?

Two ways to pay. A private charter (the whole yacht) starts from AED 2,200 per hour, with fixed prices on packaged experiences, Sunset Brunch at AED 7,500, Creator Photoshoot at AED 6,500. Or join a cruise by the seat from AED 380 per person (launch offer). Catering, crew and the full privacy protocol are always included, and we confirm a complete quote before you pay anything.

What is the cancellation policy?

Free date change up to 7 days before departure. Between 72 hours and 7 days before, one free reschedule to a date within 6 months, or a 50% refund. Within 72 hours the booking is non-refundable, though we always try to rebook you. And if we cancel, for weather, the coastguard or a shared cruise that does not reach its minimum, you choose a full refund or a priority rebooking. Full details are in our terms.

What should we bring?

Swimwear, sunscreen and whatever makes you feel good. Towels, fresh water, sunshade, a changing cabin and the prayer space are already on board. For spa and henna cruises, simply arrive, everything is prepared.

Are there prayer facilities on board?

Yes. Every yacht has a clean, dedicated prayer area with fresh mats and qibla direction indicated. The crew will gladly adjust the route timing around prayer times if you wish.
