Solo travel in Dubai: is it safe?

Short answer: yes. Dubai is consistently ranked among the safest cities in the world, and it is one of the easiest places to travel alone. Here is an honest guide to what to expect, and how to make the most of it.

How safe is Dubai, really?

Very. Low street crime, a strong police presence and a culture where public order is taken seriously mean most people feel comfortable walking, taking taxis and dining alone, day or night. As anywhere, use normal common sense, but Dubai rarely gives you a reason to feel on edge.

What to wear

Dubai is cosmopolitan and relaxed about dress in most areas: normal summer clothing is fine in malls, restaurants and beach clubs. Cover shoulders and knees when visiting mosques or government buildings. At the beach and pool, swimwear is completely normal.

Getting around

The metro is clean, cheap and easy to navigate even if you do not speak Arabic. Careem and Uber are everywhere and reliable. For anyone travelling solo, transport is one of the easiest parts of the trip.

Where to stay

Dubai Marina, JBR and Downtown are walkable, well-lit and full of cafes, which makes them comfortable bases for a solo traveller. You will always find plenty of people out and about, day and night.

How to meet other people

Solo does not have to mean alone. Group fitness classes, rooftop meetups and community events are easy ways to connect. The simplest of all is a shared sunset yacht cruise: reserve a single seat, and you spend the evening with a small group of fellow travellers and locals, often leaving with new friends. It is designed exactly for coming on your own.

A perfect solo day in Dubai

Start with a quiet morning by the pool, wander a souk or gallery in the afternoon, then join a sunset cruise to end the day on the water with new faces and the skyline glowing gold over the Marina. Safe, social and unforgettable.

The bottom line

Dubai is one of the best cities in the world to explore on your own terms. Come with an open plan, stay central, and give yourself at least one experience where you meet new people, you will not regret it.
