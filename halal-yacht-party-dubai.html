How to plan a halal yacht party in Dubai

A celebration should feel joyful, elegant and completely yours, no alcohol required and no strangers watching. Here is how to plan a halal yacht party in Dubai that everyone will remember, step by step.

What makes a celebration halal

A halal celebration simply means the event respects your values: no alcohol, halal food, and a private setting where the group can relax fully. Done well, none of that limits the fun, it makes it more personal.

Step 1: Set the date and the guest list

Pick a date that works for everyone involved, whether it is a birthday, an engagement, a reunion or just a reason to gather. Keep the list to the people closest to you, between six and twelve is the sweet spot for a warm, private day.

Step 2: Choose the venue

The venue makes or breaks privacy. Hotel suites and restaurants work, but they rarely give you the whole space to yourselves. A private yacht charter does: the entire boat is yours, the crew is warm and attentive, and at anchor the deck is calm and secluded so the group can let go completely. It is the easiest way to guarantee a truly private celebration in Dubai.

Step 3: Plan the food

Halal catering in Dubai is world-class. Think a grazing brunch, fresh mocktails and a beautiful cake. On a Bonjour Cruise charter the halal spread and mocktails are handled for you, so you plan the fun, not the logistics.

Step 4: Style it

A little styling goes a long way: a balloon arch, elegant florals, a personalised banner with the guest of honour's name. Add henna artistry for a regional, hands-on moment everyone will love.

Step 5: Capture it (privately)

Hire a professional photographer so everyone is comfortable being fully themselves. On our charters every image belongs to you only, nothing is ever used elsewhere. See our Privacy Promise for exactly how that works.

What it costs

A styled private celebration on a yacht in Dubai typically starts from around AED 12,000 for the group, depending on duration, catering and extras like henna or decor. Split between the guests as a gift to the guest of honour, it is often more affordable than a night out per head, and far more memorable.

A simple timeline

Board mid-morning or late afternoon, cruise out to a quiet bay, drop anchor for swimming and photos, serve the brunch or grazing table, do the cake and gifts, then sail back at golden hour. Three to four hours is perfect.

Not looking for the halal version? We also do a classic celebration, same warm crew, same privacy, just your choice of catering. Halal or classic, the day is entirely yours.
