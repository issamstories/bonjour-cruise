The Privacy Promise

What happens on deck, stays on deck

Most charters add privacy as an option. We built the entire company around it. Here is exactly what we do on every single cruise, written down, so you can hold us to it.

01

A warm, well-vetted crew, without exception

Captain, hostesses, therapists, photographer, every professional on board is trained, vetted and there to make your day easy. Shore staff who handle your boarding are held to the same standard. Groups are kept small and the atmosphere stays respectful and calm from boarding to disembarking.

02

Curtained mooring

When we anchor for swimming or sunbathing, we choose calm, low-traffic anchorages and raise deck screening on the exposed sides. From the water and from neighboring boats, your deck is simply not visible.

03

No-drone policy

We never fly drones over our charters, and our crew actively watches the airspace at anchor. If a third-party drone approaches, we notify you immediately, reposition the yacht if needed and report the operator to the authorities.

04

No-photo crew rule

Our crew's phones stay in a locker for the duration of your cruise. No crew member ever photographs guests. The only camera on board is the one you bring, or our on-board photographer, if you request them.

05

Professional photographer, your images only

If you want your day captured, a professional photographer joins the cruise. Every file is delivered to you through a private gallery and deleted from our systems after delivery. We never post your images, not even with permission asked nicely. Your face is not our marketing.

06

Prayer space on every cruise

A clean, dedicated prayer area with fresh mats and qibla direction is prepared before you board. Tell us if you would like the route paced around prayer times, the captain plans for it gladly.

07

Route discretion

We do not publish departure times, guest names or routes anywhere. Your booking details are shared only with the crew of your vessel, on the day itself. For public figures, an NDA-backed charter is available on request.

08

Halal whenever you want it

Choose the halal version and your menu is halal-certified and alcohol-free, nothing to inspect on your own celebration. We never serve alcohol ourselves, and the same warm, well-vetted crew looks after you either way. Halal or classic, the choice is always yours.
